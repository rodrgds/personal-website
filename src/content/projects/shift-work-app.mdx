Started as a family tool, then grew into a public Android app for Portuguese shift workers. It was called Trabalho por Turnos and now is just **ENF.**, an offline-first work companion for nurses in Portugal.

The current app is built with <Logo fixed src="expo" /> **Expo** + <Logo fixed src="react" /> **React Native**, with <Logo fixed src="sqlite" /> **SQLite** as the local source of truth and optional <Logo fixed src="convex" /> **Convex** backup and sync. It keeps the calendar-based shift management, municipal-holiday configuration, overtime/night-shift logic, and automated monthly reports, and adds a news and community feed for nurses.
