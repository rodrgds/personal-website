**OpenPost** is an open-source content workspace for solo founders. It turns launches, product updates, lessons, and ideas into content, then adapts, schedules, publishes, and tracks each destination from one place. Its destination-aware composer supports posts, threads, stories, short videos, and videos; Social Sets keep reusable account groups and format defaults; and calendar and status views show planned, published, and failed work.

The product now covers the full publishing loop: account and post analytics, rules-based native reposts, comments and replies, supported inbox collection, personal and email notifications, and a shared media library with organization and stock search. OpenPost Image Editor and Video Editor create stills and social video locally, with watermark-free exports and both quick-cut and full editing workflows.

OpenPost supports X, <Logo fixed src="mastodon" /> **Mastodon**, <Logo fixed src="bluesky" /> **Bluesky**, LinkedIn profiles and Organization Pages, <Logo fixed src="threads" /> **Threads**, Facebook Pages, Instagram Business and Creator accounts, TikTok, YouTube, and Discord webhooks. It can run as a managed service or as one <Logo fixed src="golang" /> **Go** binary or container with an embedded <Logo fixed src="svelte" /> **SvelteKit** app, <Logo fixed src="sqlite" /> **SQLite**, local media, and database-backed jobs. PostgreSQL and S3-compatible storage are optional, while the API, CLI, and MCP use scoped OpenPost tokens instead of social account credentials.
